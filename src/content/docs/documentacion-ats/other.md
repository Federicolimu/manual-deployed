## Objective
Centralize support tools such as company management, LinkedIn, and VideoAsk.

## Included modules

### Companies
Manages the client directory.
- **Tip:** Use clear public names and keep test companies hidden.
- **Error:** Duplicating companies or deleting one that has active projects.

### Connections
Manages LinkedIn connections and invitations linked to the user.
- **Use:** Filter by "Show only my connections" for personal searches.

### VideoAsk
Validates English level through asynchronous video responses.
- **Benefit:** Speeds up screening or advancement without the need for initial synchronous interviews.
- **Recommendation:** Always review videos before presenting the candidate.
