## Module Objective

Centralize administrative and complementary functionalities of the ATS BT that support the recruitment process, enabling the management of companies, LinkedIn connections, and English validations through VideoAsk.

## What is it for?

The Others module allows you to:
- Manage the companies/clients associated with projects.
- Manage and view LinkedIn connections and invitations.
- Validate candidates' English level through VideoAsk.
- Maintain structured information that directly impacts projects and candidates.

## Submodule: Companies

### Submodule Objective

Manage the list of ATS client companies, ensuring that the information is up-to-date and correctly visible for use in projects and selection processes.

### What is it for?

It allows you to:
- Create new companies.
- Edit existing information.
- Control company visibility.
- Associate companies with active projects.
- Maintain consistency between internal name and public name.

<div style="display: flex; justify-content: center;">
  <img src="/images/other/menuCompanies.png" alt="Companies Submodule" />
</div>

### Operational Tips (Companies)
- Use a clear and commercial **Display Name** for clients.
- Keep internal or test companies **Hidden**.
- Verify the website before saving.
- Avoid duplicating companies with similar names.

<div style="display: flex; justify-content: center;">
  <img src="/images/other/companiesEdit.png" alt="edit" />
</div>

### Errors to Avoid (Companies)
- Creating the same company multiple times with different names.
- Not checking visibility before using it in projects.
- Using internal names as public names.
- Deleting companies that are already associated with active projects.

## Submodule: Connections

### Submodule Objective

Manage LinkedIn connections and invitations linked to the user within the ATS BT.

### What is it for?

It allows you to:
- View existing connections.
- See sent or pending invitations.
- Filter to show only your own connections.
- Control networking reach from the platform.

<div style="display: flex; justify-content: center;">
  <img src="/images/other/connections.png" alt="Connections Submodule" />

Prospect information and the recruiter who made the connection, plus a button that redirects to LinkedIn.

### Operational Tips (Connections)
- Use this module as a reference, not as the only LinkedIn control.
- Enable the **Show only my connections** filter for personal searches.
- Review invitations before contacting duplicate prospects.

### Errors to Avoid (Connections)
- Assuming all connections are automatically synchronized.
- Not reviewing pending invitations before new contacts.
- Confusing ATS connections with personal LinkedIn.

## Submodule: VideoAsk

### Submodule Objective

Validate candidates' English level through video responses stored in real-time.

### What is it for?

It allows you to:
- Evaluate communication skills in English.
- Store responses recorded by candidates.
- Facilitate quick decisions for clients.
- Reduce unnecessary interviews.
- Support decisions to discard or advance candidates.

<div style="display: flex; justify-content: center;">
  <img src="/images/other/videoAsk.png" alt="VideoAsk Submodule" />
</div>

### Operational Tips (VideoAsk)
- Use clear and short questions.
- Evaluate fluency, comprehension, and pronunciation.
- Review videos before presenting candidates to the client.
- Document conclusions in candidate notes.

### Errors to Avoid (VideoAsk)
- Advancing candidates without reviewing the videos.
- Using VideoAsk only as an automatic filter.
- Not communicating the purpose of the validation to the candidate.
- Ignoring cultural context or initial nervousness.
