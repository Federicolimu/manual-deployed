## Objective
Activate the commercial and recruiting flow through direct contact or sending vacancies.

## Available options

### 1. Schedule a Call with Paula
Opens a **Calendly** calendar to select date, time, and time zone.
- **Tip:** Verify your time zone before confirming.

### 2. Contact us with the job position
Form to send details of a specific vacancy:
- **Company:** Name and corporate email.
- **Contact:** Name, position, and phone.
- **Vacancy:** Role name and link to description (LinkedIn/Web).
- **Additional details:** Responsibilities, tech stack, seniority, and salary range.

## Common errors
- Sending a vacancy without defined minimum requirements.
- Believing that this form automatically creates candidates (it only initiates the commercial process).
