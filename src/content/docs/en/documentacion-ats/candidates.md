---
title: Candidates
description: Management of active candidates in selection processes.
---

## Objective
Manage profiles that have already entered a recruitment flow, enabling detailed tracking and advanced searches.

## Main Features

- **Talent Charts:** Visualization by Skills, experience (Junior, Semi, Senior+), and location.
- **Detailed Filters:**
  - Role and years of experience.
  - English level and mandatory/desirable skills.
  - Country and role to update.

## Candidate Review
Each card includes:
- Name, location, and time zone.
- Match percentage (%) against Job Description.
- Access to LinkedIn, GitHub, and full profile.
- Notes section to record immediate feedback.

## Operational Tips
- Always manually validate the profile, using match percentage only as a guide.
- Document each interaction in notes to keep history visible.
- Avoid overly broad filters that make prioritization difficult.
