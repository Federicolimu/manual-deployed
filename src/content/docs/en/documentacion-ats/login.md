## Objective
Ensure secure access to the system with the corresponding permissions.

## Step by Step

1. Go to the official URL [https://bwats.betterway.dev/login](https://bwats.betterway.dev/login).
2. Enter your registered email address and assigned password.
3. Click **SIGN IN**.
4. Confirm automatic redirection to the **Dashboard**.

> **Note:** Verify that the email has no extra spaces and always use an updated browser.

## Errors to Avoid
- Repeatedly entering incorrect credentials without requesting support.
- Sharing access credentials or saving them on public devices.
