---
title: Prospects
description: Database of potential talent for searches and market analysis.
---

## Objective
Centralize professional profiles that have not yet been contacted, allowing filtering and classification before starting active processes.

## Main Features

1. **General Exploration:** Dynamic charts by Top Skills, experience, and location.
2. **Advanced Filters:**
   - **English Level:** Select required level.
   - **Skills:** Classification by mandatory (Must-have) and desirable (Should-have) skills.
   - **Country:** Specific geographic search.
3. **Prospect Card:** Shows match with vacancy, links to networks, and internal notes.

## Step by Step

1. Access the **Prospects** tab from the top menu.
2. Observe the total available and distribution charts.
3. Apply filters in the left panel according to role requirements.
4. Review resulting cards and verify match percentage.
5. Use action buttons to view the full profile or social networks.

## Operational Tips
- Clearly define **Must-have Skills** to reduce false positives.
- Use internal notes to document initial observations before contact.
- Export lists only when filters are fully refined.

## Common Errors
- Contacting prospects without first converting them to candidates.
- Ignoring English level before advancing in the process.
