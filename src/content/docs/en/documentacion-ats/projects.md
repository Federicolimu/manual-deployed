---
title: Projects
description: Centralized management of active and closed vacancies.
---

## Objective
Organize recruitment initiatives by client and position, controlling statuses and visibility.

## Module structure

The list is divided into:
- **All:** Complete history of positions.
- **Active:** Open vacancies in recruiting process.
- **Closed:** Finalized or canceled searches.

## Project detail

Each project row shows:
1. **Company:** Associated client.
2. **Name:** Position name.
3. **Prospects/Candidates:** Count and direct access to linked talent.
4. **Location:** Required location.
5. **Status & Visibility:** Operational status (active/closed) and flow visibility.
6. **Actions:** Options to edit, view details, or delete.

## Creation (New Project)
Define the profile sought, talent sources, and evaluation flows. It is the mandatory starting point to associate candidates.
