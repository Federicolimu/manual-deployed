## Objective
Monitor all system activities, including personal assignments and automated executions.

## Task Categories
- **User Tasks:** Pending, completed, in progress, postponed, and failed.
- **Agent AI:** Tasks where AI makes assignment and execution decisions (see image 3).
- **System:** Predefined automated processes without autonomous decision-making (see image 5).

## Daily Use
Serves to verify that there are no blocks in automated flows and to ensure your manual responsibilities are covered.
